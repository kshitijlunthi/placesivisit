Uttarakhand Trek Guide

Uttarakhand is known as the land of the Himalayas and offers some of the most beautiful trekking routes in India. From snow covered peaks to lush green valleys, the region is a paradise for adventure lovers.

Popular Treks in Uttarakhand

  • Kedarkantha Trek
  • Valley of Flowers Trek
  • Roopkund Trek
  • Har Ki Dun Trek

Best Time to Visit

The best trekking seasons in Uttarakhand are from April to June and September to November when the weather is pleasant and the mountain views are clear.

Travel Tips

Always check weather conditions before starting a trek and carry proper trekking gear including warm clothing, trekking shoes and sufficient water.
